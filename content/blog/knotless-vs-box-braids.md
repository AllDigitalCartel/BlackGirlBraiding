This is the question we’re asked most often, and the honest answer is that neither style is better. They solve different problems. Here is how to work out which one is yours.

## The actual difference

A **box braid** starts with a small knot that anchors the extension hair to your own at the root. A **knotless braid** has no knot at all. We begin braiding your natural hair and feed the extension in gradually over the first inch or so.

Everything else follows from that one difference.

## Tension and your edges

This is the real reason knotless braids took over.

The knot in a traditional box braid sits at the root and pulls. On a strong hairline that’s fine for years. On a fine or already-stressed hairline, repeated over many installs, it’s how traction alopecia starts.

Knotless braids distribute the weight along the braid instead of concentrating it at one point. If your edges are thin, if you have had tension damage before, or if you simply find braids uncomfortable for the first few days, choose knotless.

## Weight and comfort

Knotless braids are lighter and lie flatter against the scalp. They also move more naturally from day one, where box braids typically need a few days to settle.

If you’re getting a long style, waist or butt length, the weight difference is significant. Long box braids are noticeably heavier on the neck.

## Definition and the parting

Box braids win here. The knot holds the section tightly, so the square partings stay crisp and the root looks full. If you want that sharp, geometric box-braid look, particularly with larger braids, the knot is doing real work.

Knotless roots read softer. Most people prefer that; some don’t.

## Install time and cost

Knotless braids take longer, because feeding the hair in gradually is slower than tying a knot. That time is what you’re paying for.

## So which one?

**Choose knotless if** your edges are fine or previously damaged, you want a long style without the weight, you find braids uncomfortable at first, or you want the softest, most natural-looking root.

**Choose box braids if** you want a crisp, defined parting, you have a strong hairline, you’re working to a tighter budget, or you specifically want that classic silhouette.
